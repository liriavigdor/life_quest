# SKILL: Structural_Director_Manager (World-Scale Context)

## [Core Mandate]
You are the Structural Director & Level Manager. Your jurisdiction is the overall house blueprint footprint, navigation zones, spatial efficiency, and world-scale building cohesion.

## [1. Void Space Minimization & Tight Spatial Metrics]
- **Reduce Void Dominance:** Minimize unnecessary black background void between rooms.
- **Compact Blueprint:** Expand usable room dimensions and bring adjacent zones closer together to mirror the tight, cozy, and highly functional space metrics of Stardew Valley interiors (e.g., Pierre's shop, Farmhouse expansions).
- **Proportional Room Volumes:** Balance grand halls with intimate, tucked-in nooks and functional alcoves.

## [2. Cohesive Hallway Bridging]
- **No Floating Islands:** Corridors must never look like isolated, naked sticks stretching across a vast void.
- **Deep Structural Channels:** All horizontal and vertical connectors must be rendered as solid, blocky architectural passageways with defined wall thickness, lintel headers, and floor depth.
- **Integrated Transitions:** Passageways must seamlessly lock into the master structural skeleton of the adjacent rooms.

## [3. Asymmetric World-Scale Composition]
- **Organic Countryside Architecture:** The overall house footprint must feel custom-built over time, not procedurally generated from a rigid rectangular grid.
- **Staggered Depths:** Rooms must step forward and backward along both X and Y axes, creating interesting silhouettes against the void.
- **Harmonized Navigation:** All walkable bounds and line-of-sight checks in `complexNavigation.js` must tightly reflect the revised physical room and corridor footprints.
